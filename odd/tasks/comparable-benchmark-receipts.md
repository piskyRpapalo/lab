# COMMISSION · BEACON · comparable benchmark receipts

**PROBLEM** · A map building cannot honestly compare device results when the
underlying task and measurement conditions are unknown or different.

**OUTCOME** · Compare two supplied receipts only when their declared workload,
parameters, metric, units and measurement method match; explain missing or
mismatched evidence without inventing a score.

**AFFECTS** · Lab receipts and future app/map benchmark views.

**CONSTRAINTS** · Offline standard library; no benchmark execution, private
identifiers, prompts, raw model output or network. A metadata match is not
independent validation of a run.

**OPEN QUESTIONS** · Which real labelled runs and device privacy policy will
be approved for the first end-to-end building demonstration?
